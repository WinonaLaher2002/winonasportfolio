My Design Philosophy

Inspired by pastel and limited color palettes, cozy themes, handwritten elements, and nature visuals. I work intentionally with two to three color combinations to create balanced, calming, and cohesive designs.

Clean, semantic HTML and well-structured CSS
Responsive, accessible, and user-friendly layouts
Strategic color palette planning for cohesion
Strong typography and visual hierarchy awareness
Campaign-based thinking for social media content
Attention to detail in both code and creative execution

Hi! I'm Winona B. Laher, also known as LuckyLeaf. I'm a front-end developer and visual designer who enjoys creating soft, aesthetic, and functional digital experiences.

I blend clean code with thoughtful design to build interfaces and visual content that feel smooth, intuitive, and emotionally engaging. My work extends beyond websites; I also design social media campaigns, themed content series, and digital graphics that maintain brand consistency and strategic color harmony.

I believe good design should not only look beautiful, but also communicate clearly and connect meaningfully. Every line of code I write and every visual I design is part of my growth journey as a creative developer.
